[View Project](https://github.com/lisarigo1c-source/electricity-load-forecasting-italy)

Master's thesis comparing statistical methods for day-ahead electricity demand forecasting in Italy. Implemented multiple-seasonality models in R and evaluated their forecasting accuracy and prediction intervals.

[View Project](https://github.com/lisarigo1c-source/alternative-fuel-vehicle-choice)
